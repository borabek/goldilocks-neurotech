# Model card: v2 (spectral-feature model)

**Files:** `results/v2/frozen_model.joblib` (SHA-256 in `results/v2/freeze_record.json`),
code in `v2_model.py` and `src/v2_evaluate.py`.
**Status:** research model, frozen on 7 November 2026. Not a medical device and not for clinical use.

## What it does

Labels each 30-second epoch of a two-channel forehead EEG night as W, N1, N2, N3 or REM, and adds up
total sleep time. For every epoch and channel it computes 14 spectral and amplitude features (log
band powers, relative band powers, spectral entropy, log standard deviation, log mean absolute
difference), adds 5- and 15-epoch context, and passes them to a gradient-boosted tree classifier. The
two channels' probabilities are averaged and smoothed with a hidden-Markov step whose weight was
chosen on six selection people.

## Training data

BOAS headband recordings (OpenNeuro ds005555), 48 fitting people, AASM consensus labels. Six other
people chose the smoother weight and six more calibrated the probabilities. The 20 test people were
not used for any choice.

## Performance

Mean over people, whole recording. Full table with intervals: `results/paper/benchmark.json`.

| Dataset | People | Kappa | Macro-F1 | Wake recall | Sleep-time error (min) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BOAS test (same headband) | 20 | 0.661 | 0.561 | 0.858 | 31.8 |
| Sleep-EDF (Fpz-Cz, 23-hour recordings, inspected during development) | 20 | 0.579 | 0.478 | 0.747 | 235.9 |
| DOD-H (frontal PSG, pre-registered test) | 25 | 0.111 | 0.215 | 0.142 | 57.5 |
| DOD-H with the q75 gain calibration | 25 | 0.318 | 0.375 | 0.349 | 46.2 |
| HMC (F4-M1, clinical, pre-registered test) | 25 | 0.331 | 0.387 | 0.760 | 48.8 |
| HMC with the q75 gain calibration | 25 | 0.243 | 0.299 | 0.914 | 130.5 |

YASA 0.7.0, never trained on the headband, does better on every dataset except BOAS.

## Known failure modes

- **Amplitude.** The features depend on absolute amplitude. On a recorder whose signals are several
  times smaller (DOD-H) the model falls apart. A label-free gain estimated once per dataset repairs much
  of that, but on a recorder that is already close (HMC) the same correction pushes REM into wake and
  makes things worse. Use it only when the estimated gain is far from 1.
- **Long recordings.** On recordings that include daytime wake it scores part of the day as sleep
  (236 minutes too much on Sleep-EDF). The sleep-window detector in `analysis/sleep_window_detector.py`,
  applied only to recordings longer than about 14 hours, cuts this to 158 minutes.
- **N3.** BOAS has little N3 (3.6% of scored epochs), so the model under-calls it everywhere. On BOAS
  it sends 41% of reference N3 to N2.
- **Confidence.** Its top probability is a poor guide to correctness off the headband: on HMC its
  most confident epochs are wrong more often than its epochs overall.

## Intended use

Research on headband sleep staging and on how models fail under device change. Do not use it for
diagnosis, treatment decisions or any individual's care.
