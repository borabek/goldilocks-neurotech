# Idea 2 (NeuroBridge): additional in situ hydrogel materials

Compiled 2026-11-07 from 5 web searches. Adds to `idea2-in-situ-hydrogel-materials.md` and `idea2-parkinsons-in-situ-hydrogels.md`. Only materials not already covered there are listed in full; overlaps are flagged.

**Already in the project (not repeated):** poloxamer 407/188, carbopol, HPMC, chitosan-based nasal gels, PNPHO, starch nanoparticle + carboxymethyl chitosan, PEG-pHPMAm-lactate + thiolated HA, self-assembling peptide gels, HAMC, Diels-Alder HA (xHA), collagen + 4s-StarPEG, laminin gel, tannic acid-gold chitosan, activin B thermogel, GelMA (Alzheimer's doc).

**How far to trust it.** Abstracts, review excerpts and repository pages only (status A). Numbers are as reported. Most entries are brain-injury, stroke, spinal cord or retina models, not intact-barrier delivery, so they say little about nose-to-brain uptake.

## 1. New material families

### 1a. Ion-triggered and pH-triggered nasal gels (no temperature trigger)

| Material | Example and result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deacylated gellan gum (DGG), Ca2+-triggered | Harmine nanocrystals in 0.5% DGG gel: brain bioavailability 25x oral nanocrystals; lower acetylcholinesterase, more acetylcholine in brain (Huang 2021, Int J Pharm 594:120182) | A |
| Gellan gum, ion-triggered nanoemulgel | Naringin self-nanoemulsifying system in gellan gel: higher drug transport and brain targeting efficiency than comparators (Pharmaceutics 2021;13:1216, PMC8400950). Gels from nasal mucosal secretions | A |
| Gellan + xanthan | Resveratrol lipid-carrier gel: 5x nasal permeation vs suspension gel; better memory than oral suspension in rats (Rajput 2018) | A |
| Gellan gum + lipid carrier | Donepezil NLC gel: better scopolamine-amnesia performance and brain distribution than marketed product; lower plasma level (Asian J Pharm) | A |
| Thiolated xyloglucan, pectin, EHEC (ethyl hydroxyethyl cellulose) | Named as nasal in situ gelling agents in the Agrawal 2020 review (J Control Release 327:235), which covers poloxamer, chitosan, EHEC, xyloglucan, carbopol, gellan and DGG. Pectin also appears in the rasagiline Pluronic gel in the Parkinson's doc | A (review list) |

Design notes from the sources: chitosan needs acidic pH to dissolve and can precipitate or irritate at nasal pH, so HPMC is often preferred as the mucoadhesive (Singh 2025 risperidone paper). Gellan and xanthan gums are described as effective but may be costly (Xie and Xie 2024 review).

### 1b. Injectable thermoresponsive polymers for the brain, spinal cord and eye

| Material | Example and result | Site and model | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PLGA-PEG-PLGA triblock | Strong gel at 25% w/v at 31-34 C; dexamethasone and ketorolac released over 47-62 days; 84.5% cell viability, near 100% with drugs added (López-Cano 2021, Pharmaceutics 13:234). Also intramuscular curcumin depot in AD rats (Alzheimer's doc) | Intravitreal (retina), in vitro | A |
| Poly(organophosphazene), imidazole-modified "I-5" | Near-complete elimination of cystic cavities and better tissue repair in contusive spinal cord injury (Nat Commun 2017, PMC5599609) | Rat spinal cord | A |
| PNIPAAm-g-PEG | Motor and sensory recovery after hemisection; no heightened inflammation by IL-1beta, IL-6, TNF-alpha at 15 days (Bonnet 2019, Mater Sci Eng C) | Rat spinal cord, 12 weeks | A |
| Heparin-poloxamer | Enhanced GDNF effects on circuit remodeling and neuroprotection after injury (Zhao 2017, J Biomed Mater Res A) | Rat spinal cord | A (title in a reference list) |
| Chitosan + beta-glycerophosphate | Nasal D-penicillamine gel in APP/PS1 mice (Alzheimer's doc); chitosan scaffold with bone marrow stem cells improved motor function in spinal cord injured mice (Zhang 2020) | Nose; mouse spinal cord | A |
| Methylcellulose + collagen | Temperature-responsive; supports encapsulated stem cell viability in vitro (Payne 2016) | In vitro | A (title in a reference list) |

Handle with care (carried over): PNIPAAm monomer toxicity, high-concentration poloxamer 407 in brain tissue.

### 1c. Shear-thinning and self-healing physical gels

| Material | Example and result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guest-host hyaluronic acid (cyclodextrin + adamantane) | Injectable, shear-thinning, self-healing; HA content tuned to match brain tissue stiffness (J Mater Chem B 2026, accepted 2 June 2026, doi prefix 10.1039/d5tb02805a) | A; materials paper, no animal data in the excerpt |
| Hyaluronic acid-phenylboronic acid + dopamine-modified gelatin (HA-PBA/Gel-Dopa) | Mimics composition, stiffness and viscoelasticity of brain tissue; rapid hemostasis, tissue adhesion, self-healing; supported neural cell infiltration, reduced astrogliosis and glial scar, closed lesions in mice (Hu 2022, Adv Healthc Mater) | A |
| Shear-thinning hydrogels with immobilised bidomain peptide and PDGF | Delivered human oligodendrocyte progenitors to shiverer mouse brain; less cell death, more myelinating oligodendrocytes, better myelination at 12 weeks | A |
| Engineered shear-thinning gels with different surface charges (Nat Commun, PMC7718896) | In healthy and stroke-injured mouse brain, nonionic and anionic gels gave minimal foreign-body responses and better molecular delivery; cationic gels drew stromal cell infiltration | A |
| Supramolecular peptide hydrogels (patent US 11,661,439) | Folded-peptide fibril gel; shear-thins in the syringe and re-forms at the target; claimed for protein (IL-7) and cell delivery | Patent, low evidence |

Material-design rule worth using: charge of the gel surface changes the brain reaction (cationic worse), which fits the earlier note that cationic materials are the high-risk end.

### 1d. Stimulus-responsive and other gels

| Material | Example and result | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ROS-responsive PVA crosslinked with alginate-phenylboronic acid (Alg-PBA) | Releases glutathione when oxidative stress is high; best at 2:1 Alg-PBA to PVA; 100 ug/mL glutathione reduced neuronal apoptosis under oxygen-glucose deprivation (J Biomater Appl 2025;40:181) | A; in vitro |
| Microbial transglutaminase-crosslinked soy protein isolate | 10% SPI with 20 mg MTGase per g SPI: storage modulus about 260 Pa, close to brain; for nanoparticle (liposome) release; brain tumour context (Ciprandi 2026, J Mater Sci Mater Med, preprint) | A |
| Light-responsive hydrogels | Patent for stem cell differentiation in light-responsive gels for neuro disorders (US 9,234,171) | Patent |
| Electro-responsive, magnetic, ultrasound, enzyme-triggered gels | Listed in the Panseri and Montesi 2022 review of bioactive injectable CNS hydrogels (Acta Biomater) | A (review list) |
| UNAM nanogel | Nanoparticle network that supported neuron growth in animal studies; news reports only | News, low evidence |
| Triglycerol monostearate-hyaluronan-NRG1 | Neuroinflammation-responsive gel, intracerebroventricular, AD mice (Alzheimer's doc) | A |

## 3. Screener use (suggestion)

Add a "gelation trigger" value for each: temperature, ion (Ca2+ or Na+), pH, shear-thinning (physical), self-assembly, chemical crosslink, ROS-, light- or enzyme-triggered. Add "surface charge of gel" (cationic, nonionic, anionic) because it predicted tissue reaction in the mouse study above. Keep tumour, stroke, spinal cord and retina rows separate from intact-barrier nose-to-brain rows.

## 5. Open items

- Open the Bellotti 2021 and Agrawal 2020 reviews for gelation temperatures, concentrations and safety notes per polymer.
- No ADHD or ADHD-like disease cargo found with any of these gels beyond atomoxetine and methylphenidate in poloxamer systems.
- Not searched: alginate-calcium injectable brain gels, elastin-like and silk gels, hyaluronan-based nasal gels, polysaccharide gels for peptide cargo, gel toxicity beyond 14 days.
