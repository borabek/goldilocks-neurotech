The result that decides the pitch

The CNN trained tonight is the best model on the headband and the worst on Sleep-EDF: kappa 0.728 → 0.086, sleep-time error 16.5 min → 803.2 min, calling daytime wake sleep. Choosing a wearable model by its in-domain score would have picked the one that fails hardest away from home. That is the argument for inspectable evaluation rather than a single accuracy number.

The demo, in three acts

Decode a held-out night → reveal the human reference and the YASA lane and challenge the evidence → inspect the resulting summary, its error and what it cannot say. Asked for lights-off sleep-onset latency, the viewer refuses: the recording does not establish lights-off, so it offers recording-start to first scored sleep instead. The viewer itself is precomputed retrospective analysis. Live inference is a separate, measured path: live_inference.py scores epoch by epoch with trailing-window normalisation, and costs 0.038 kappa against the same model scored retrospectively (0.728 → 0.690), with no lookahead.

Main limitation

Prediction intervals missed their 90% target (60% coverage on BOAS, 20% externally), so calibrated-range claims are disabled in the release gate and exported intervals are null. The BOAS cohort was inspected at v1, so it is a development comparison, not independent confirmation. No clinical, ADHD, medication or paediatric claim is made.

Where the work is, and how to check it

No public repository, by team decision: the artefact is the folder Goldilocks_Submission/ on the team machine, 72 MB, with code, derived features, every result, the viewer and the backup video. Reproduce the frozen evaluation with:
python3 -m venv .venv .venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt .venv/bin/python reproduce_v2.py
31 seconds, no network. Verified from a clean environment: 294,208 numbers compared, largest relative difference 2.6 × 10⁻¹⁵, nothing differing at four decimal places (REPRODUCTION.md).
